# Project Impact

MerhabaMap helps people with Turkish roots in Germany discover places, events and community resources in German and Turkish.

## Current reach

- launched in June 2026
- approximately 700 registered users during the first month
- coverage of more than 100 German cities
- maintained by a small two-person team

## Ecosystem gap

Local discovery is often fragmented across closed social groups, word of mouth and monolingual services. MerhabaMap provides a structured, culturally relevant and privacy-conscious discovery layer while developing reusable patterns for bilingual UX, moderation, consent and city-level location handling.

Figures above describe the project’s early launch period and are provided for context rather than as real-time analytics.
