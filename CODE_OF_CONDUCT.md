# Code of Conduct

MerhabaMap is committed to a welcoming, respectful and constructive contributor community.

Be considerate, discuss ideas rather than people, respect different backgrounds and avoid harassment, discrimination or intimidation. Community work may involve language, identity, migration and culture; approach these topics with care and good faith.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive contributions and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to **info@merhabamap.com**. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports.
